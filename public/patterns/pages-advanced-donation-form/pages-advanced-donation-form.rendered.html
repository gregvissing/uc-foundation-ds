Common questions

        		

        			

        				
What will my donation support?

        				

        					
You may designate your gift to any area of the university or UC Health. You can use this form to divide your gift among several funds and choose one-time or recurring donations.

        				

        
        				
What are the areas I can support?

        				

        					

        						
You can choose individual funds to support in one of four areas.

        						
UC Funds
Support the greatest needs across the university.

        						
Colleges/units
Support funds specific to individual colleges or units.

        						
UC Health
Support centers and departments related to UC Health.

        						
Scholarships
Support students across all of UC.

        						
Athletics and UCATS
Support programs across UC athletics.

        					

        				

        
        				
What if I don't see my area of support?

        				

        					
Under Choose an area to support, select Search fund by name to enter the name of the fund you are looking for.

        				

        
        				
Are gifts tax-deductible?

        				

        					
Gifts made without receiving goods or services are generally fully tax‑deductible. If goods or services are received, only the portion of your gift above their fair market value may be deductible. We ultimately advise that you consult your tax professional.

        				

        
        				
Does UC accept matching gifts?

        				

        					
Yes. Click here to find out if your employer has a matching gift program. You will be given a space to enter their name during the checkout process.

        				

        
        				
Is there a minimum gift amount?

        				

        					
Yes. The minimum gift amount is $5.

        				

        
        				
What payment methods are accepted?

        				

        					
For online giving, we accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover, Diners Club, JCB). You can explore other ways to give, including check, legacy giving and stock exchange.

        				

        
        				
How should I notify you of a gift sent through a donor-advised fund, IRA, ACH or stock transfer?

        				

        					
If you are making a gift through a donor-advised fund, IRA distribution, ACH or stock transfer, please email giftadmin@foundation.uc.edu. Please include your name, the gift type, what fund you are giving to and how to properly recognize you. That will allow us to properly acknowledge your generosity and ensure your gift is directed according to your wishes.

        				

        
        				
I am a UC or UC Health employee. Can I make a gift via payroll deduction?

        				

        					
Yes. UC and UC Health employees can give via payroll deduction.

        				

        
        				
I have a question. Who can I contact?

        				

        					
If you have questions about making a gift, please call 513-556-6781 or email us at giftadmin@foundation.uc.edu.
